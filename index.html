Full Paper

GEMR:KG — A Knowledge Graph for Global Emerging Markets Risk Analysis

19th IADIS International Conference on Information Systems (IS 2026) — Zagreb, Croatia

Unified three siloed World Bank datasets — Global Economic Monitor, Global Emerging Markets Risk Database, and Worldwide Governance Indicators — into a single OWL ontology served over a SPARQL endpoint.

Built an ontology-aware natural-language-to-SPARQL service in Python and FastAPI, with embedding-based IRI grounding and a self-healing retry layer that recovered 10.9% of failed first attempts.

Designed the 73-question benchmark used to evaluate the system: 78.1% answer accuracy against 5.5% for the strongest of seven baseline LLMs, at 2.5s average latency.
